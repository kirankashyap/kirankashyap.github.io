A Glimpse of Kiran

Agile change agent and AI enthusiast. That is the short version of what I do. I am also a DevOps enthusiast who enjoys figuring out how all the moving parts of a project fit together.

I help everyone from engineers to leaders use AI to make their daily work a little easier and more productive. I approach every challenge with a systems thinking mindset because I like looking at the big picture to find what works best for the whole team.

I have spent time in small startups and big organizations. That mix taught me that regardless of company size the goal is always the same. It is about clearing away the clutter so people can actually enjoy their work and do their best.

When I am not working with teams you will usually find me on a badminton or snooker court. I also spend a lot of my free time reading Swami Vivekananda books.
