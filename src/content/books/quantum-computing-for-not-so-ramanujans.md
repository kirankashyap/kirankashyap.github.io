Most introductions to quantum computing assume you are comfortable with linear
algebra before page one. This book is for readers who are not, and who still
want to understand what a quantum computer does.

It works through qubits, superposition and entanglement, then the algorithms
built on them, then the real applications. The last stop is a practical one:
how to tell which quantum computing claims are hype.

Written with Vivek Ganesan and Kamal Raj Sekar. Coming soon.
