Many teams measure the wrong things: velocity for its own sake, burndown charts
nobody reads, dashboards nobody acts on. This illustrated handbook sorts the
metrics that predict delivery and market outcomes from the ones that waste a
team's time.

It draws on years of running forecasting and reporting for product teams at
startups and enterprises, and covers the whole lifecycle, from early
forecasting to checking whether a release landed with customers.
