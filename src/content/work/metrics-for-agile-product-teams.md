Most teams measure the wrong things — velocity for its own sake, vanity burndown
charts, dashboards nobody acts on. This illustrated handbook lays out which
metrics actually predict delivery outcomes and market success, and which ones
quietly waste a team's time.

Drawing on years of running forecasting and reporting for product teams at
startups and enterprises, it covers metrics across the full lifecycle: from
early forecasting through to measuring whether a release actually landed with
customers.
